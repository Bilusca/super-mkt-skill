# Channel formats

Read only the section for the requested channel.

## Meta Ads

Deliver, with genuinely different angles (not variations of one idea):

- 5 Hooks
- 10 Headlines
- 5 Primary Texts
- 5 CTAs
- 5 Descriptions

Angles to vary: pain, desire, common enemy, proof, curiosity, identity, contrast, news.

## Google Ads

- 15 Headlines (max 30 characters each — count them)
- 4 Descriptions (max 90 characters each — count them)
- Sitelinks
- Callouts
- Structured Snippets

Search intent outranks creativity. Mirror the query.

## Landing Page

Build in conversion order:

Hero → Proof → Benefits → How it works → Differentiators → Testimonials → Offer → Guarantee → FAQ → CTA.

The hero carries promise + mechanism + CTA above the fold. Repeat the CTA at every decision block.

## Email

- 10 subject lines
- 5 preheaders
- Full email
- Strategic PS (the PS is the second most-read element — put the promise or the scarcity in it)

## Instagram

Hook (first line, before the "more" cut) · Caption · Storytelling · CTA · Relevant hashtags.

## LinkedIn — organic posts

Deliver:

- 5 Hooks (first 2 lines only — that's all that shows before "see more")
- Full post, 900–1300 characters for engagement-led, up to 2500 for authority-led
- 3 CTA variants (comment bait, DM trigger, link in first comment)
- A carousel outline (8–10 slides) when the topic supports it

Rules for the platform:

- Line breaks every 1–2 sentences. Walls of text die in the feed.
- The hook must work with zero context — no "as I mentioned", no dangling pronoun.
- No hashtag spam. Three, maximum.
- No external link in the post body when reach matters; put it in the first comment.
- Voice is first person and specific. A number, a date, a named mistake. Vague thought-leadership reads as AI and gets scrolled.
- Formats that work: contrarian take with proof, teardown of a real example, before/after with figures, lesson from a specific failure, process breakdown.

## LinkedIn Ads

Specify the format first — Single Image, Carousel, Video, Document, Message/Conversation Ad, or Lead Gen Form — then deliver:

- 5 Intro Texts (first 150 characters carry it; the rest truncates)
- 10 Headlines (keep under 70 characters, hard cap 200)
- 5 Descriptions (under 70 characters)
- 5 CTAs matched to LinkedIn's button set (Learn more, Register, Download, Sign up, Request demo, Get quote)
- For Message Ads: subject line + body under 500 characters + one CTA
- For Lead Gen Forms: form headline, privacy blurb, and the thank-you message

Angles: role-specific pain, cost of the status quo, peer proof from a recognizable company, industry benchmark, risk of doing nothing.

B2B rules: targeting is by job title and company, so name the role in the copy. Ticket is high and the buying committee is real — write to the champion who has to defend the decision internally, not only to the person clicking.

## SEO / blog and organic landing pages

Include: primary keyword, secondary keywords, search intent, H1, H2, H3, meta description, slug, FAQs, suggested internal links, optimized CTA.

Never sacrifice readability for keyword density.

## VSL / video script

Hook (first 5s) → Promise → Story/credentials → Problem and amplification → Unique mechanism → Solution → Proof → Offer → Bonuses → Guarantee → Scarcity → CTA → Final objections → CTA again.

Mark approximate timestamps and visual cues where useful.
