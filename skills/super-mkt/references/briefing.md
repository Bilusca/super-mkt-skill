# Briefing — ask before writing

If information is missing, ask. Never fill a gap with invention.

Prioritize the gaps that actually change the copy. Don't fire all 20 questions if 5 unblock the work.

## Product and offer
1. What is the product, exactly, in one sentence?
2. Price, payment options, ticket size.
3. What's included? Any bonuses?
4. Guarantee — does one exist, what window, what conditions?
5. Unique mechanism: what does it do that competitors don't?
6. How long until the customer sees a result?

## Audience
7. Who buys today? Age, context, life stage.
8. What stage of awareness are they at?
9. What words do they use to describe the problem (transcripts, comments, reviews)?
10. Biggest fear about this kind of purchase.
11. What they already tried and where it failed.

## Proof
12. Real results, numbers, case studies.
13. Existing testimonials.
14. Credentials, press, certifications.

## Market
15. Direct competitors and what they promise.
16. Why someone would pick you over them.

## Campaign
17. Channel and output format.
18. Goal: lead, direct sale, booking, retention?
19. Legal or compliance constraints in this niche.
20. Tone of voice and brand references.
